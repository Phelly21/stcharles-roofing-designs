Start with the problem, not the product

Most people who call us aren't shopping for a material. They have a stain on the ceiling, shingles in the yard after a storm, or a roof that is old enough to make them nervous. So we start there.

  • Something is leaking now. Start with roof repair. Most leaks come from flashing, pipe boots, valleys or a few lifted shingles, and many can be fixed without replacing the roof.
  • A storm just came through. Read storm and hail damage, then our plain-English guide to your rights in a Missouri hail claim.
  • The roof is old and you want to know where you stand. A roof inspection tells you what needs attention now and what can wait.
  • It is time for a new roof. See roof replacement, then compare architectural shingles, metal roofing and stone-coated steel.

Choosing a roofing material in St. Charles

Our weather narrows the field. St. Charles County sees regular spring hail, summer heat, and winters that swing above and below freezing, so the right roof has to handle impact, wind and moisture, not just look good from the curb.

MaterialTypical lifespan*Best for
Architectural shinglesAbout 30 yearsMost homes; the best value per year of service
3-tab fiberglass shinglesAbout 20 yearsBudget re-roofs, rentals, matching an existing roof
Metal (standing seam)40 to 80 yearsLong-term owners, low-slope porches, modern designs
Stone-coated steelDecades; manufacturer warranties are often lifetime limitedHail-prone roofs where you want the look of shake or tile

*InterNACHI estimated-life figures. Real lifespan depends on ventilation, installation and weather.
