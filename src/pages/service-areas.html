Why the rules change from town to town

St. Charles County is a patchwork. The City of St. Charles generally doesn't require a building permit to replace shingles, but its six local historic districts have design review. St. Peters publishes its own re-roofing requirements, which call for a permit in some cases, such as replacing a large share of the sheathing or changing to a different roofing material. Unincorporated St. Charles County generally doesn't require a permit to replace a roof, but it does require a floodplain development permit for reroofing a structure in the regulated floodplain, which matters in Orchard Farm, Portage des Sioux and West Alton.
