While you wait

  • Active leak? Move valuables, put a bucket under the drip, and if the ceiling is bulging, poke a small hole in the center of the bulge so the water drains into the bucket instead of bringing the drywall down. Don't go up on a wet roof.
  • After a storm? Photograph damaged shingles in the yard, dented gutters and downspouts, and any ceiling stains. Dated photos help if you file a claim. Read your rights in a Missouri hail claim.
