What happens after you reach out

  1. We call you back and set a time. We'll ask what you're seeing (a leak, storm damage, an aging roof) and find a time to come out.
  2. We look at the roof. We check the shingles or panels, flashing, vents, valleys, gutters and the attic where we can get to it, and take photos so you can see what we see.
  3. You get a free written quote. It spells out what we found, what we recommend, and the materials we'd use. If a repair will do, we'll tell you.
Dealing with a storm claim?

Missouri law gives you specific rights when an insurance claim pays for your roof, including a five-business-day window to cancel a roofing contract after a claim denial. Read your rights in a Missouri hail claim before you sign anything with anyone.
