## Unincorporated St. Charles County (also governs Weldon Spring by contract)
- County Building Permit Information page lists "Replacing a roof" under projects that "generally … do not require permits" (read from Wayback 2026-02-11; live page moved).
- BUT floodplain: county Floodplain Development Permit page — floodplain permits "are required even in cases when a building permit would not. For example, … replacing a window, … or reroofing a structure all require a floodplain development permit" (floodway, floodway fringe, density floodway on 2016 FIRMs). https://www.sccmo.org/1605/Floodplain-Development-Permit
- Substantial damage/improvement 50% rule: if repair cost of a substantially damaged structure ≥50% of pre-damage market value, structure must be brought into compliance (lowest floor ≥1 ft above base flood elevation). https://www.sccmo.org/1608/Substantial-Damage-Improvement
- Codes: 2021 I-codes w/ amendments; 2021 St. Charles County Residential Code. Table R301.2: ice barrier NO; 107 mph; 20 psf; weathering Severe. Drip edge eaves + rakes mandatory. Roofing/siding replacement exempt from smoke/CO alarm upgrades.
- The county does not license roofers as a trade.
- County Building & Code Enforcement: 636-949-7345.

## 3. Weldon Spring  (slug: roofing-weldon-spring-mo)
- Pop. 5,326 (2020); 7.78 sq mi; platted 1864; median age 53.1. Community Development 5401 Independence Rd.
- Near Missouri River bluffs, along Hwy 94 and near I-64/US 40-61 [roads common knowledge; fine].
- History (DOE): WWII Weldon Spring Ordnance Works — "the largest explosives factory in the United States" (TNT/DNT), closed Aug 15, 1945; AEC uranium plant 1955–1966; DOE cleanup late 1980s–2001 → 41-acre, 75-ft disposal cell with a public viewing platform; Weldon Spring Site Interpretive Center (2002); 8-mile Hamburg Trail links to Busch Memorial Conservation Area, Weldon Spring Conservation Area, Katy Trail. Say "near Weldon Spring".
- City parks: Liberty Pavilion, Founders Pavilion by the city lake, Freedom Pavilion, Independence Amphitheater, the Red Barn, Huber Park.
- Subdivisions: Whitmoor Country Club (36-hole course, association); Camelot; Enclave. Higher-end homes on golf & estate lots.
- City ordinance: front façade of residential buildings must be brick or stone unless the Architectural Review Commission approves otherwise → large, complex, steep roofs, brick-wall flashing. City ARC reviews new home exteriors; reroof review [UNVERIFIED].
- Weldon Spring Heights: separate village, pop. 93 (2020), off Hwy 94 — mention only.
- Schools: Francis Howell R-III (Independence Elementary, Bryan Middle).
- Permits: city contracts with St. Charles County Building & Code Enforcement and uses county codes → county rule (replacing a roof generally no building permit); city land-use permit applies to many projects — for re-roof [UNVERIFIED] → "check with the city's Community Development office".

## 4. Portage des Sioux  (slug: roofing-portage-des-sioux-mo)
- Pop. 335 (2020); 0.68 sq mi; ZIP 63373. Founded 1799 (Spanish Lt. Gov. Zenon Trudeau and François Saucier). 1815 Treaties of Portage des Sioux signed here. On the Mississippi, roughly opposite Elsah, IL. Orchard Farm R-V schools.
- Access: north from St. Charles on Hwy 94, then Route J.
- Our Lady of the Rivers Shrine: after the 1951 flood spared the town, residents dedicated a 25-ft fiberglass statue of Mary on a 20-ft pedestal (Oct 13, 1957); annual July Blessing of the Fleet. St. Francis of Assisi Parish.
- Floods: 1993 record crest 38.17 ft at Grafton gauge (Aug 1, 1993), water reached every street; June 2019 ~34.7 ft, streets impassable, church basement flooded; county buyout notices list 1973, 1993, 2011, 2019 floods.
- 2019-flood buyout: county-run, ~$15.7M federal (HUD CDBG-DR), up to ~100 homes, 900+ homes flooded, voluntary, land kept as open space; covers unincorporated, Portage, West Alton, parts of St. Charles city; wraps up by early 2027.
- Roofer angles: open river-bottom wind exposure; marina/river lots; substantial-damage 50% rule when a roof is part of a larger post-flood repair; floodplain permits. City has a floodplain ordinance; building permit authority [UNVERIFIED] → "call the city".

- Mayor (AP/PBS, June 2024): many remaining homes have been raised; buyouts cleared nearly all homes on Ely Street and areas exposed to Bear Creek. [Mayor's 3,900-in-1970 figure unverified — omit.]
- Landmarks: Confluence Point State Park (Edward "Ted" and Pat Jones–Confluence Point), Audubon Center at Riverlands, MDC West Alton & Dresser Island areas; city parks: West Alton City Park, Memorial Park with Veterans Memorial; Rivers Pointe Fire Department.
- Permits: city posts a Land Use Permit form and a Floodplain Development Permit form whose development types include "Routine Maint" and "Min Improvement" → "contact City Hall before any exterior work; floodplain permits may apply".
- Roofer angles: elevated homes on piers → taller access/staging; wind exposure; insurance/FEMA substantial-damage interplay.
